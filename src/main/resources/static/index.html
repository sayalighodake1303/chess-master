📖 How to Play Chess Master

🟢 Legal Move Highlighting

Click on any of your chess pieces. The application immediately calculates all legal moves according to official chess rules and highlights the valid target squares in GREEN.

  • Green Circle: Legal destination square.
  • Green Ring: Legal piece capture.

♟ Piece Movements

♙ Pawn: Moves 1 square forward (or 2 on its first move). Captures diagonally 1 square forward. Supports En Passant and Pawn Promotion.
♘ Knight: Moves in an "L-shape" (2 squares in one direction, 1 square perpendicular). Can jump over other pieces.
♗ Bishop: Moves any number of vacant squares diagonally.
♖ Rook: Moves any number of vacant squares horizontally or vertically.
♕ Queen: Combines the power of Rook and Bishop in all 8 directions.
♔ King: Moves 1 square in any direction. Can perform Castling with a Rook.

⚡ Special Rules Supported

  • Castling: Move King 2 squares towards Rook if neither has moved and intervening squares are safe and empty.
  • En Passant: Capture an enemy pawn that just moved 2 squares forward adjacent to yours.
  • Pawn Promotion: Reach the 8th/1st rank to promote to Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.
  • Check & Checkmate: A king under attack is highlighted in Red. If no legal move can escape check, it is Checkmate!
